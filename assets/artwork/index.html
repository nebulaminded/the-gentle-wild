“Each stroke is a decision,
each color a memory of light,
each shadow a knowing
that darkness is never empty.”

— from Lisa’s poetry archive

About Lisa

Painter, poet, observer of the gentle wild.

Lisa Trexler-Fallon creates paintings and poems shaped by moonlight, tenderness, memory, myth, and the emotional language of nature.

Her work explores the connection between wilderness and softness — where animals become mirrors, flowers become memory, and beauty becomes a quiet act of courage.
