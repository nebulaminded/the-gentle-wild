About Lisa

Painter, poet, observer of wilderness, tenderness, memory, moonlight, and the emotional language hidden quietly within the natural world.

The Gentle Wild

Lisa Trexler-Fallon creates paintings and poems that live between softness and strength — where animals become mirrors, flowers become memory, and beauty becomes a quiet act of courage.

Her work draws deeply from nature, mythology, tenderness, grief, longing, romance, and the sacred emotional atmosphere found in moonlight, wilderness, and stillness.

Through image and language, The Gentle Wild invites viewers into a world where softness is not weakness, and where the natural world becomes a place of emotional remembrance.
